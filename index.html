THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES

Chess is a conversation.

You and your opponent take turns moving one piece. Your goal is checkmate: attack their king so it has no legal escape.

  1. Choose a white piece. Dots show legal destinations.
  2. Choose a destination. Read the coach’s preview, then play the move.
  3. Keep your king safe. “Check” means it is under attack and you must respond.
  4. Experiment freely. Use a hint or take back your last turn.

Castling, promotion, en passant and draw rules are handled for you. Start with Learn step by step to meet each piece.
